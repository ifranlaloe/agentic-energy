# Energy offers

This document contains the stable product facts for the three Northwind Energy plans.

---

## Flex

**Tagline:** Freedom to change

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Price type | Variable (market-linked) |
| Contract period | No fixed term |
| Estimated monthly cost | €138 |
| Early exit fee | None |
| Renewable electricity | No |

**What it means for customers:**
The energy price follows the market. In cheaper periods customers pay less; in more expensive periods they pay more. There is no commitment: customers can leave at any time without a penalty. Suitable for customers who value flexibility over price certainty.

---

## Zeker

**Tagline:** Price certainty

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Price type | Fixed |
| Contract period | 1 year |
| Estimated monthly cost | €129 |
| Early exit fee | €75 |
| Renewable electricity | No |

**What it means for customers:**
The energy price is fixed for one year from the contract start date. Monthly costs are predictable regardless of market movements. If a customer leaves early, they pay a €75 exit fee. Suitable for customers who want to know exactly what they will pay each month.

---

## Groen

**Tagline:** Renewable electricity

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Price type | Fixed |
| Contract period | 1 year |
| Estimated monthly cost | €142 |
| Early exit fee | €75 |
| Renewable electricity | Yes (100%, verified certificate) |

**What it means for customers:**
Identical to Zeker in structure (fixed price, 1-year term, €75 exit fee) but the electricity is sourced from renewable generation and backed by a green certificate. The estimated monthly cost is slightly higher than Zeker, reflecting the renewable sourcing. Suitable for customers who want a fixed price and care about renewable sourcing.

---

## Notes

- All estimated monthly costs are based on average household consumption of approximately 3,000 kWh/year and are illustrative only.
- Actual costs depend on the customer's own usage.
- Prices are fictional and do not reflect real market rates.
- No plan is objectively the best — each involves trade-offs the customer must weigh for their own situation.
